Please:
1. Sign in with the account above (email login), or use Sign in with Apple / Google
2. Open My leagues (bottom tab)
3. Open "PT Test BVB 2's" (public Beach Volleyball 2's sample)
4. Also available: "PT Test Gin Stats" and "PT Test Chess Stats" (unlisted)
5. Check Standings, year filter, Games, player profiles, and Share
6. Optional: New league tab -> create a Sports or Game night league
7. Optional scores: when adding a game, leave score fields empty and save a win/loss only
8. Faster add game: open PT Test BVB 2's, tap +, confirm first winner is focused with suggestions and score chips
9. Appearance: Settings tab -> pick a theme (try Classic or a dark look), then reopen a league
10. Account deletion: Settings tab -> Account -> Delete account (two-step confirm).
   Do not use the demo account for a live delete test if you still need it;
   create a throwaway account to verify the flow if needed.
