Why PlayTracker?

  • ★
    One league object

    Sports, cards, and board games live in the same list. Mix a Tuesday night crew however you want.

  • ✓
    Fastest game entry

    Name the winners, drop a score if it matters, and the standings move. Team names work for indoor volleyball.

  • ◎
    Clean on purpose

    Glass cards, a cotton candy gradient, and stats that stay out of the way until you need them.

  • ♛
    Built from real games

    Started as beach volleyball standings. Now it covers chess, checkers, Monopoly, and whatever you add next.

About PlayTracker

PlayTracker is for people who already keep score on a napkin. Make a league, invite the group, and keep a running record of who won. Public leagues are easy to share. Private ones stay in the crew.

The original volleyball stats still live at playtracker.org/arbel. Same data, same 404 ball, now on Railway instead of PythonAnywhere.
