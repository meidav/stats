About PlayTracker

PlayTracker is the simple way to keep score for the people you actually play with. Make a league, log a game in a few taps, and watch standings build themselves.

It is for beach volleyball on Sundays, chess on the patio, checkers with the kids, and the board-game night that somehow always ends in an argument about who is winning. One app, one list of leagues, every kind of play.

Why it exists

Most of us already keep score. On a napkin, in Notes, in a group chat that nobody can search. PlayTracker is that habit, cleaned up: winners, losers, a score if it matters, and a history you can look back on.

The idea is not another spreadsheet. It is the fastest path from "we just played" to "here is the table." Public leagues are easy to share. Private ones stay in the crew.

What you can do

Create a league, pick a sport or a table game, and start logging. Standings update as you go. Player pages show win rate, streaks, and who you keep beating. Download PlayTracker on the App Store and that same league is in your pocket.

Google Play is on the way. Until then, you can browse public leagues here in your browser.
